- Unobserved physical alternatives can be averaged into uncertainty.

        The project avoids weak moves such as bigger models, generic uncertainty, or a benchmark-only contribution. It centers a mechanism-level change: Tactile pretraining negative transfer keeps action-critical alternatives explicit until a physical observation collapses them.
