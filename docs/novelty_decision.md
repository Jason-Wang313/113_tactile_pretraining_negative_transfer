# Novelty Decision

Decision: STRONG_REVISE

The v5_expanded rebuild carves a clearer novelty boundary than the archive draft and the v4.1 continuation package. The paper is not "tactile pretraining helps" or "uncertainty improves transfer." It studies negative transfer from tactile source pretraining and proposes a control-conditioned guard that rejects harmful pretrained channels while retaining clean-transfer benefits.

The local evidence supports this boundary against a broad comparator set, including frozen pretraining, full fine-tuning, domain-adversarial transfer, invariant-risk transfer, uncertainty gating, ensemble disagreement, conformal risk control, sensor-health filtering, test-time adaptation, masked/contrastive tactile pretraining, and the retained v4 guard. The claim remains incomplete without real robot or accepted high-fidelity evidence.
