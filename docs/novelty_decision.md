# Novelty Decision

Decision: STRONG_REVISE

The v4.1 rebuild carves a clearer novelty boundary than the archive draft. The paper is not "tactile pretraining helps" or "uncertainty improves transfer." It studies negative transfer from tactile source pretraining and proposes a control-conditioned guard that rejects harmful pretrained channels while retaining clean-transfer benefits.

The local evidence supports this boundary against frozen pretraining, full fine-tuning, domain-adversarial transfer, uncertainty gating, and ensemble disagreement filtering. The claim remains incomplete without real robot or external high-fidelity evidence.
